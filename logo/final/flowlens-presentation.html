The brief

Process discovery and improvement software for small and medium businesses. The identity should feel clear, approachable and professional - easy for non-technical teams, never intimidating.

Success: recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour, avoids the magnifying-glass-over-flowchart cliche, and reads as both an F and a process flow.

  • Reads as the letter F and as a tiny flowchart - no explanation needed
  • Rounded, friendly strokes suit non-technical SME teams
